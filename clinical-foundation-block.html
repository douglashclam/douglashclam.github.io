Go back through your notes and the lecture slides, not the videos.

Sat 26 Sep — Medicine: CNS + GI/abdomen
Content review

CNS: cranial nerve lesions, motor/sensory patterns, level of consciousness scales — these lend themselves well to MCQ/EMQ. GI: abdominal exam findings and their clinical significance, lab test interpretation. Try writing a couple of short-answer explanations from memory — that's SAQ practice.

Sun 27 Sep — O&T + MSK + vascular
Content review

Mon 28 Sep — O&G + Paediatrics + ENT + Ophthalmology

Sun 4 Oct — MBBS IV formative: MCQ/EMQ + SAQ
Dissertation restructured to steady daily pace. Analysis and remaining lit review are quick, so the real bottleneck is the 10,000 words — spread at ~350/day across every day (not just weekends) from now to 1 Sep, in the order Methods → Results → Intro/Lit review → Discussion, with the final week (26 Aug–1 Sep) reserved for conclusion, abstract, references, and a full read-through rather than new quota. This is a much gentler daily load than the earlier weekend-only crunch plan, which is why the "dissertation crunch" framing on weeks 4–6 has been dropped — it's steady, not a fire drill. The dorm move weekend (22–23 Aug) can now absorb a missed day or two without real risk to the deadline. Everything else — bedside/PBL/logbook priorities, exam-video pacing, block lectures, and the week 11 formative revision — stays as before.
