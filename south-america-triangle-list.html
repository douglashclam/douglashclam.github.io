The honest framing: South America is overwhelmingly a nature continent at the top tier — its ▲▲▲s are Patagonia, Iguazú, Galápagos, Uyuni, Rio's setting — plus the one singular human-built summit, Machu Picchu, and the remote enigma of Easter Island. Unlike Asia there's no dense Old-World heritage layer; the reward is landscape, wildlife, and a handful of extraordinary cities and ruins.

The most "you" thread: the Inca-and-altiplano arc — Cusco → Machu Picchu → Titicaca → Uyuni → Atacama — strings the continent's deep-history and most surreal landscapes into one high-altitude spine, the closest South America comes to a cultural-heritage through-line.
