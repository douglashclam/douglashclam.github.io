The honest cut on China: the unconditional ▲▲▲s — defendable to anyone, no asterisk — are the Forbidden City, Terracotta Army, Mogao, Great Wall, Li River, and Lhasa. The rest carry a real caveat: interest-dependent (grottoes), weather (Huangshan), conditions (Jiuzhaigou), or a managed/crowded experience (Zhangjiajie, restored on landform merit).

The spine that actually fits you: the eastward transmission of Buddhist monumental art — Longmen → Yungang → Mogao in China, then Ellora & Ajanta in India, with Bagan and Borobudur as the bookends.

Ratings calibrated in the Steves triangle idiom on an everything-counts basis (spectacle, food, atmosphere, landscape — not heritage alone). Live logistics — visas, closures, security, opening times — change often; verify against current sources before booking.
