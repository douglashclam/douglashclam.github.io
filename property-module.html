Core concepts
Bullish vs Bearish Now: Bull
Reading market direction
Bullish ↑
Market expects prices to rise. Buy sooner, negotiating power weaker, leverage works in your favour. Waiting costs you.
Bearish ↓
Market expects prices to fall. Waiting is rewarded. Buyer has negotiating power. HK fell 28% peak to trough 2021–2024.
Signals to watch
CCL index direction, transaction volume, new launch sell-through rates, HIBOR trend. Price up + volume up = genuine bull. Price up + volume thin = fragile.
Time-series vocabulary
WoW · MoM · QoQ · YoY · YTD
WoW — Week over Week
Used for CCL index, fast-moving data. Noisy, easy to over-read.
MoM — Month over Month
RVD price index releases. Can look dramatic but may just be seasonal noise.
YoY — Year over Year ★ most useful
Smooths seasonality. Chinese New Year always distorts Jan/Feb MoM figures. YoY is the reliable signal.
QoQ / YTD
Quarter over Quarter. Year to Date = cumulative from Jan 1.
Rich Dad Poor Dad framework
Assets vs liabilities, cashflow mindset
Core distinction
Asset = puts money in your pocket. Liability = takes money out. Your home is a liability. A rental property generating positive cashflow is an asset.
Cashflow Quadrant
E (Employee) → S (Self-employed) → B (Business owner) → I (Investor). Goal: migrate toward I. Most people never leave E.
HK application
Classic middle-class HK path = buy one flat to live in, pay it off, retire. Kiyosaki's path = use leverage, buy income-generating property, use cashflow to finance the next asset. HK is structurally ideal for this model.
Caveats
Vague on execution, US-centric tax advice, Kiyosaki himself has had financial controversies. Take the mindset, not the specific tactics.
HK mechanics
Stamp duty
First-time buyer advantage is significant
First-time buyer rate: 4.25%
On a HK$5M flat = ~HK$212K. Use this once — it disappears the moment you own any property.
Standard rate: 15%
On a HK$5M flat = ~HK$750K. That's HK$538K saved by buying as a first-timer. Massive.
2025 Budget change
Flat duty of HK$100 now applies to properties up to HK$4M (raised from HK$3M). Easier entry on lower-value units.
HKMA mortgage rules
LTV, stress test, deposit requirements
LTV (Loan-to-Value)
First-time buyers can borrow up to 90% on properties under HK$10M under the mortgage insurance programme. 10% deposit = ~HK$500K on a HK$5M flat.
Stress test
Bank assesses if you can afford repayments at +2% above current rate. As a student with no declared income, this is the main obstacle. Bank looks at YOUR income, not your mum's.
HIBOR-linked rates
Most HK mortgages are HIBOR + spread. 1M HIBOR currently ~2.1%. Total mortgage rate ~3.25–3.5%. Monthly repayment on HK$4.5M loan at 30yr ≈ HK$20–22K/month.
Guarantor structure
Mum can act as guarantor (not co-borrower) — gives bank comfort on repayment using her income, while keeping her off the title deed. Preserves your clean first-time buyer status.
Personal strategy (Douglas)
Family structure, timeline, key numbers
The structure
Buy in my name (first-time buyer 4.25% SD). Mum provides deposit capital as documented family loan + covers mortgage interest while I'm in training. I take over payments once earning as a doctor.
Why the clock is ticking
Mum retires in ~10 years. Banks get uncomfortable co-signing near retirement age. Her peak income is the asset — borrow against it while it exists.
Target timeline
Buy in 2–3 years (clinical year 1–2), not 5+. Enough declared income to help with stress test, mum still peak earning, bull market not fully played out.
Upfront cost estimate (HK$5M flat)
10% deposit ~HK$500K + stamp duty ~HK$212K + legal/misc ~HK$60K = ~HK$800K total cash needed. Monthly mortgage ~HK$21K.

District analysis
Kai Tak, Kowloon, Northern Metropolis
Kai Tak — mixed verdict
New stock, harbour views, Sports Park open. But 12,866 units in pipeline 2023–26, only ~5,649 absorbed by Nov 2025. 52%+ mainland buyers = concentration risk. Better as 5yr+ hold than near-term flip.
Kowloon City / Ho Man Tin / To Kwa Wan ★
Same East Kowloon upgrading trend as Kai Tak but cleaner supply fundamentals. Less flashy, better risk/reward for near-term purchase.
Northern Metropolis
900K+ units planned, adjacent to Shenzhen. 10–20 year GBA bet. Low entry, thin liquidity now. Speculative but potentially huge.
Mid-Levels / The Peak
Capital preservation. Strong rental demand from expats + Top Talent Pass arrivals. Weak yield (~2–3%), high entry. For later in life.
